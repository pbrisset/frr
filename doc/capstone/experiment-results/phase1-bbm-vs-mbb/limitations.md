# Limitations

- Monotonic events are command-completion or sampled observations. There is no netlink monitor, no measured capture realtime-to-monotonic offset/uncertainty and no exact bgpd/Zebra internal timestamp. BGP capture time must not be used to infer exact ordering against the harness clock.
- Attachment overlap and BBM disconnection are bracketed by userspace completion observations; IP/default-route transfer is several commands and is not atomic. The harness adds a Zebra query during MBB overlap, extending short overlaps and adding measurement load. Normal IP egress is configured on only one interface at a time.
- Forwarding snapshots are slow and sequential. The sampled host /32 absence, /24 presence and inferred lookup are separate observations. No exact stale-state duration or per-packet causal path is established. No access-interface packet correlation has been performed for lost probes.
- Continuous ICMP covers one flow and limited points in time; recorded timeout and RTT are not exact network outage durations. Packet captures use Linux `any`, potentially duplicate observations across interfaces, and capture loss has not been certified. BGP packet/NLRI counts are per device and direction, not unique fabric events; TCP reassembly can affect the decoder.
- Initial and final state checks do not certify every transient or prove distributed convergence after restoration. Flags and sessions are sampled; unobserved transitions cannot be excluded. Only one seed and one fixed execution order were used, with five trials per setting. CPU and observer effects are not independently controlled or quantified.
- The comparative SVG displays selected event observations; it is not a continuous state diagram. Per-run timelines have separate monotonic and BGP realtime panels.
