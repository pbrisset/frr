# Controlled reproduction

Nine sequential fresh topologies ran the same single-veth BBM Leaf1→Leaf2 move using the fixed 2-spine/5-leaf fabric, host11 `00:00:00:00:00:11`/`45.0.0.11`, and baseline DAD enabled (5 moves/180 s, captured `show evpn` in every `events.jsonl`). All nine pytest invocations passed their harness checks, which do **not** require post-move forwarding recovery. Each has one move. Seeds 0 and 1 change a one-element shuffle and therefore do not create meaningful independent traffic randomness; they do identify distinct fresh runs. The phase0 harness primes all hosts, probes continuously from the controller at requested 10 Hz, and always runs background control traffic. None is a no-traffic environment. Treatment offsets are from the worker's start, after new-attachment completion and an old-leaf snapshot, not from the exact physical link transition. The worker records send timestamps. `any` captures can duplicate frames.

See `controller-probe-results.csv` for realized sends/s and failure bursts, `intervention-comparison.csv` for sample counts. A loss is a probe timeout/error, not a measured outage length; the runs with no recovery are right-censored at the capture/probe end. Per-move observations here are 9/9 missing /32 and 9/9 wrong lookup sampled, with loss in 7/9, under these specific fixed workloads and timing. They are descriptive, not estimated general probabilities or evidence of statistical significance. Two treatment runs per group are insufficient for reliable rates. Route snapshots are sparse, sequential and non-atomic; absence between snapshots is unobserved and restoration is bounded, not exact.

The gateway intervention was added after the failure pattern was observed. The ping succeeded; more importantly `raw-gateway-02/host11.pcapng` frames 97–98 and `leaf2.pcapng` frames 88–97 record a gateway ARP request for the robot and the robot's reply, at realtime 1793764415.12747–.12750. Leaf2 netlink then logged the robot neighbor `REACHABLE` at 03:53:35.127560; Leaf2 BGP captured MAC/IP announcements (see CSV), Leaf5 netlink recorded /32 installation via 10.0.0.2 at 03:53:35.290889, and snapshots later resolved Leaf2. Controller replies returned, but a separate delayed-ARP run proves reply recovery does not require restored /32. Thus this sequence supports an association between a real local neighbor exchange and restoration of the MAC/IP route, not a proof that the ping itself or only one of its frames is the necessary cause. No neighbor deletion or FRR change was used as an intervention.

The raw worker logs record Python send/command calls; captures record actual observed ARP. The immediate treatment emits a gratuitous self-target ARP, **not** a gateway ARP exchange. Periodic telemetry invokes ICMP ping; subprocess timeout/scheduling can lengthen its intended interval, and `host11-stimulus.log` records actual executions. No AP model was reproduced because its source and trace were not located. Phase 1 comparisons are historical different traffic/handoff cases, not this statistical population.
