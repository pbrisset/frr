# Fleet findings

No raw AP run, narrative report, or implementation was located. The listed 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 205, and 1,000-robot scales; mean 60, 30, 10, 5, 2, 1 s dwell; synchronized/staggered scheduling; and three traffic treatments are **unverified**, including whether all were actually run. The matrix records dimensions individually, not an invented Cartesian set of runs. Requested and realized rates, completed moves, seeds, probes, convergence, UPDATE counts, queues, CPU and memory are unknown for each. No statistics or scale threshold can be recalculated. The historical seven-row workload includes two robots, but is not the AP study: `tests/topotests/bgp_evpn_capstone/audit_2026-10-20/REPORT.md` documents serialized link operations, polling, 3.400 and 3.852 s reported timings and post-return DAD warnings. It cannot validate an AP fleet claim or characterize scale.

BGP UPDATE message counts are per peer and direction; a message may carry multiple advertised and/or withdrawn NLRIs. An NLRI transmission is not a unique route change, and repeated advertisements, captures at multiple points, and multiple peers must not be summed as distinct changes. TCP retransmissions and capture duplication need separate identification. A per-peer decoder should report message and byte rate, NLRI-per-message distributions, unique route keys/actions and sequence changes, capture drops, and TCP retransmissions. Output queues require time series after load stops: occupancy alone is not sustained overload; drain slope, offered and completed load, session counters and error/reset logs matter. Low average CPU does not establish headroom; inspect per-core peaks, scheduling, memory and queueing.

No evidence here shows movement outpacing convergence, BGP batching under AP synchronized load, or a 1,000-robot breakdown. Achieved workload may differ from requested; compare synchronized and staggered at matched measured rate and burst profile, not fleet size alone. Probe sends and return timestamps must establish realized sampling rate and loss; validate generator saturation and observer overhead. Record namespace and shared Linux neighbor/FDB limits and actual per-namespace sysctls/counters before attributing failures to FRR. The Phase 0/1 small emulation results do not establish production switch capacity.
